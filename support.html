A few useful answers

How do we start?

Choose a topic or a Daily Challenge on Home. Normal and Advanced are practice bands, not standardized assessment levels. The app interface and read-aloud are currently in English.

How does the Mistake Notebook work?

A wrong answer adds or reopens a question. On a fresh visit, a correct first answer without hints moves it to Practised. Corrections during the same visit and hinted answers keep it ready for another try.

Does question feedback reach the developer?

No. Expand Question feedback · Grown-ups below a question to leave a local note. In the gated grown-up space, Saved feedback and export manages notes and exports a JSON file through the system file picker. A cloud destination may synchronize that file.

How do I remove records?

Reset learning progress in the grown-up space; question feedback is kept. Clear a feedback note and deselect all its tags to remove that entry. Delete App removes local app data. Backups and exported files must be managed separately.

What if a save fails?

Keep the app open and check available device storage. For feedback, use Retry saving or try exporting. Unreadable original files are preserved. Avoid resetting or deleting the app before considering the effect on your records.

How can I report a puzzle or layout issue?

Use the public support channel with the question ID, expected and actual behavior, app version and device model. Crop screenshots to the question and redact private information. Do not attach full learning records or feedback exports.
